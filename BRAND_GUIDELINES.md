# Business Conversion Fulcrum — Brand Guidelines

**Parent:** Aristocrat Intelli-Tech.

**Commercial name:** Business Conversion Fulcrum (BCF) — Your Hyper-Niche Revenue Catalyst Partners.

**Value proposition:** You focus on execution. We find the right buyers. Together, we work to close deals.

**Philosophy:** Survival of the Fittest: adapt to changing customers and consistently improve commercial execution.

**Primary colours:** Obsidian Graphite `#10151B`; Titanium Slate `#303B46`; Rose Copper `#C88F7D`; Signal Teal `#27C7B8`; Porcelain White `#F4F2EE`; Deep Steel `#18252E`.

**Identity:** Technical, premium, architectural precision; a mechanical fulcrum rather than generic growth-arrow iconography. Existing `assets/logo-mark.svg` is a provisional symbol pending approved master brand asset. Do not imply registered trademark status without validation.

**Voice:** Direct, understandable, commercially precise. Explain problems as finding the right buyers and converting/retaining them. No invented conversion guarantees or testimonials.

**Public disclosure:** Do not identify participating clients without express consent. Describe discretion without falsely claiming military or government certification.

**User data:** The current qualification form relies on visitor-controlled WhatsApp sharing, not silent data collection.

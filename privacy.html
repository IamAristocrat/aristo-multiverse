Privacy & Confidentiality

Business Conversion Fulcrum · By Aristocrat Intelli-Tech

Our approach: We treat the identities of prospective and participating strategic partners as private. We do not use client participation, names or project information in Instagram promotion, public showcases, public case studies or other promotional displays without specific written permission. Our operations are built around discretion and mutual consent.

Information we ask for at introduction

The public questionnaire requests a contact name, organisation, industry, operating geography, non-sensitive description of services, desired buyer groups, commercial priority, timing and optional callback number. It does not request revenue, margins, customer lists, confidential agreements or trade secrets.

How the introductory form works

The initial form is processed within your browser. The site does not upload or store these form entries on its own server. After completion, it prepares a message for you to review and voluntarily send using WhatsApp. WhatsApp is a separate service with its own privacy and data-processing practices. If you do not send that message, we do not receive the form answers. You may instead call +91 98945 95035.

When a partnership begins

An enquiry is not an agreement or acceptance. Strategic partnerships are considered individually. A formal engagement begins only when both parties mutually agree to the scope, responsibilities, fees, authorised actions, confidentiality expectations and other material terms.

Confidentiality and public references

We do not disclose the identity or participation of a strategic partner through public marketing without express written authorisation. More detailed protections—such as access limitations, information exchange, conflict controls, handling responsibilities, retention and deletion—must be defined in the relevant written agreement. Defence-minded discretion is a statement of our intended discipline, not a claim of military affiliation or government security accreditation.

Website operation

Hosting providers may process ordinary technical logs, including IP addresses and request metadata for operating and protecting the website. This release does not intentionally use advertising pixels, cross-site marketing trackers or an on-site customer database. If these arrangements change, this notice should be reviewed and updated before deployment.

Your choices

Do not submit information that you are not authorised to share. You can request clarification or a private discussion by calling +91 98945 95035. If information is provided through an external messaging provider, that provider's own controls and terms also apply.

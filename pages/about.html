About Us

A legacy of excellence, a future of impact

Our Story

Founded in 1994 by Dr. Alexander Whitmore and a small team of visionary consultants, Aristo-Multiverse began with a simple but powerful belief: that the world's most complex challenges require insights that transcend traditional boundaries.

What started as a boutique strategy firm in London has grown into a global leader with over 2,500 professionals across 45 countries. Throughout our journey, we have remained committed to our founding principles: intellectual rigor, client impact, and the relentless pursuit of excellence.

Today, we serve over 500 of the world's leading organizations—from Fortune 500 companies to sovereign wealth funds, from healthcare giants to technology disruptors. Our work has helped create more than $50 billion in value for our clients.

As we look to the future, we continue to push the boundaries of what consulting can achieve, combining cutting-edge research, advanced analytics, and deep industry expertise to help our clients navigate an increasingly complex world.

Our Journey

Three decades of transformative impact

1994

The Beginning

Aristo-Multiverse founded in London with a team of 12 consultants and a focus on strategy and corporate finance.

1999

European Expansion

Opened offices in Paris, Frankfurt, and Zurich, establishing our presence across continental Europe.

2005

Global Reach

Expanded to New York, Singapore, and Dubai, becoming a truly global firm with 1,000+ professionals.

2012

Digital Transformation

Launched our Digital practice, helping clients navigate the Fourth Industrial Revolution.

2018

AI & Analytics

Established our Advanced Analytics Center of Excellence, integrating AI into all our service offerings.

2024

Sustainability Leadership

Committed to net-zero operations by 2030 and launched our Sustainability & ESG practice.

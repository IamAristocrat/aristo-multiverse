03 / IMPLEMENTABLE TECHNOLOGY

A beautiful structure is only the beginning.

Every project begins with site reality, regulatory boundaries and a defensible engineering specification. The application and context determine whether a capsule, smart module or adaptive concept is appropriate.

Real-world commissioning entails defined responsibilities, inspected fabrication, electrical safety, documented controls and site acceptance. We do not claim that a concept is delivered merely because it can be rendered.

01 — Structural system and foundation

Load path, anchorage, geotechnical assumptions, wind loads, transport geometry, local code review.

02 — Envelope and moisture strategy

Thermal bridges, glazing, vapour control, coastal exposure, waterproofing and service access.

03 — Electrical, HVAC and life safety

Power distribution, safe isolation, heating and cooling, ventilation, fire and egress compliance.

04 — Controls and cyber-resilience

Network and role separation, commissioned monitoring, control fallback and data minimisation.

05 — Fabrication and acceptance

Drawing release, quality checks, off-site tests, transport constraints, commissioning and handover.

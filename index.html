THE GRANDVISTA JOURNAL

Craft, materials
& commercial clarity.

Practical design knowledge for serious residential decisions — addressing both spatial beauty and the engineering that determines long-term value.

01 / KITCHENS

Plan a kitchen around the way you actually cook.

Map wet and dry preparation, safe countertop clearances, ventilation, concealed services, drawer loading and the real maintenance consequences of surface choices.

02 / WARDROBES

Storage is a system, not a façade.

Define hanging lengths, seasonal storage, dresser ergonomics, drawer mechanisms, ventilation, lighting access and the reliability of hardware before finishes.

03 / ESTIMATION

How to compare two turnkey quotations.

Review measurable scope, board grade, hardware SKU, labour inclusions, taxes, waste allowances, protection, milestones and the treatment of variations.

04 / MATERIAL INTELLIGENCE

Stone, engineered boards and architectural films.

Choose by application. Review heat, moisture, installation tolerance, edge behaviour and documented warranty rather than relying on showroom adjectives.

05 / LIGHTING

Layers that change the experience of a room.

Design ambient, task and accent lighting together with accessible drivers, colour consistency, maintainable wiring and appropriate glare control.

06 / PROJECT EXECUTION

From approved design to disciplined handover.

Use drawing revisions, material sign-off, snag records, photographic evidence and maintenance documentation to protect quality after installation.

08 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we work together.

Are you a traditional lead-generation agency?

No. We research buyer fit, identify relevant opportunities, coordinate conversations and, within an agreed mandate, help progress deals. We distinguish researched prospects from genuine sales-ready opportunities.

Do you guarantee sales or conversions?

No. We agree scope, responsibilities, service activities and measurable milestones. No legitimate partner can guarantee every independent buyer decision.

Will our name or projects appear in public promotions?

Not as part of our normal operating model. We do not identify participating strategic clients on Instagram, public displays, marketing showcases or case studies without specific written mutual consent.

Do we need to reveal sensitive business details immediately?

No. The introductory questionnaire asks only what you offer, who you want to reach, your market and where you need help. Sensitive information is considered only if genuinely necessary and subject to an agreed handling process.

How do fees and additional costs work?

Pricing is discussed after understanding your industry, workload, buying cycle and scope. Agreed service fees and third-party costs—such as advertising, licensed data or travel—are set out separately before any commitment.

Who handles the final sale and delivery?

BCF can support buyer contact, follow-up and deal progression within agreed authority. Your team retains responsibility for technical delivery, agreed approvals and customer commitments unless expressly contracted otherwise.
